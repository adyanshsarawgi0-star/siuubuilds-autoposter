Privacy Policy for siuubuilds-autoposter

Effective date: November 25, 2026

siuubuilds-autoposter is a private, single-user application operated by the owner of the SIUUBUILDS (@siuubuildz) YouTube channel. This application uses YouTube API Services.

1. YouTube API Services & Google Privacy Policy

By using this application, users acknowledge and agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

2. Google & YouTube Data Accessed

When authorized by the channel owner via Google OAuth 2.0, the application requests only:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — to upload original YouTube Shorts created by the owner to the owner's channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — to read subscriber, view, like, and comment counts for the owner's own channel and videos.

3. Data Storage, Sharing, and Retention

  • OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's private computer only (~/.autoposter/).
  • No personal data is collected from external visitors or third parties, and no YouTube API data is sold, shared, transferred, or used for advertising or AI model training.
  • Retrieved channel statistics are stored locally in plain Markdown/JSON files solely for the channel owner's review.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Deletion & Revoking Access

In addition to our normal deletion procedure, the owner may revoke this application's access to Google/YouTube data at any time via the Google Security Settings / Permissions page (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) and delete all locally stored tokens and cached stats files immediately from their computer.
