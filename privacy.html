Privacy Policy for siuubuilds-autoposter

Effective date: November 25, 2026

siuubuilds-autoposter is a private, single-user application operated by the owner of the SIUUBUILDS (@siuubuildz) YouTube channel.

1. Data We Access

When authorized by the channel owner via Google OAuth 2.0, the application uses only:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — to upload original YouTube Shorts to the owner's channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — to read view, like, and comment counts for the owner's uploaded videos.

2. How Data Is Stored and Used

  • OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner's private computer only.
  • No user data is collected from public visitors, and no data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third parties, advertisers, or AI training datasets.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Deletion and Revoking Access

The owner may revoke access at any time from Google Account Permissions and delete the local token files from their computer.
